My Social Contract

My Commitments & Action Plan

Below are my binding commitments to the Rosebank International student body. Click "Read Action Plan" on any commitment to view the implementation strategy.

Commitment 01

Visible & Accessible Leadership

Staying visible, approachable, and responsive beyond election season.

Implementation Breakdown:

  • Weekly Open Office Hours: Setting aside fixed weekly hours where any student can walk in to discuss academic or campus concerns.
  • Direct Communication Channels: Maintaining active WhatsApp and digital channels for rapid grievance logging.
  • Regular Campus Walkthroughs: Weekly informal campus walkthroughs to engage with students directly outside formal meetings.
Commitment 02

Open & Transparent Communication

Publishing monthly updates, financial oversight reports, and event schedules.

Implementation Breakdown:

  • Monthly Performance Digest: Publishing transparent digital reports highlighting SRC achievements and ongoing negotiations.
  • Budget Transparency: Clear breakdowns of campus event expenditure so students see exactly where resources go.
  • Open Town Halls: Quarterly feedback sessions prior to major SRC policy votes.
Commitment 03

Action-Oriented Student Feedback

Turning survey results and student concerns directly into trackable solutions.

Implementation Breakdown:

  • Issue Ticketing System: Logged grievances will be assigned priority status and tracked until resolution with management.
  • Student Pulse Surveys: Monthly quick polls on campus facilities, cafeteria standards, and academic support needs.
  • Escalation Guarantee: Unresolved issues will be brought directly to campus management with formal SRC backing.
Commitment 04

Unwavering Integrity & Student First

Zero ego, ethical leadership, always putting student welfare first.

Implementation Breakdown:

  • Servant Leadership Ethos: Treating leadership as a public trust, prioritizing student academic and personal success above personal ambition.
  • Fair Representation: Equal advocacy for students across all departments, years, and backgrounds.
  • Ethical Conduct Code: Strict adherence to honesty, accountability, and respectful dialogue with both students and administration.
